About Me

Director, Developer & Platform Experience · Moody's Analytics

17 years building production systems at scale — the last three as AI Architect and now Director, owning the engineering platform that helps hundreds of developers build, ship, and operate software.

My focus is AI infrastructure: RAG pipelines, LLM evaluation frameworks (Recall@k, groundedness, MRR), agentic workflows, and the cloud architecture that runs them in production. I hold a Master's in Data Science from UC Berkeley (GPA 3.81).

I host AI Builders: LatinX Edition — a podcast featuring researchers and practitioners shaping the future of AI — and teach RAG, embeddings, and LLM fundamentals at Techqueria workshops. I've spoken at Techqueria Tech Summit, Oakland Tech Week, and the Latino AI Summit.
